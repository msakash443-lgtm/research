Questions for the walkthrough (orange pins on the screen)

  1. Q1 Where do I approve? G4 opens only when every critical (★) cell is checked by a person. Is the bar at the top where you'd look? Should a supervisor also be able to check cells, or only approve?
  2. Q2 Where do I see the source? Click any cell: the quote is shown highlighted on its page next to the value. Is that enough, or do you need the real PDF page image?
  3. Q3 How do I go back? "Back to table", Esc and the browser Back button all return to the table with your place kept.
  4. Q4 Are the four cell states clear? Is "Not reported" different enough from "Rejected"?
  5. Q5 Rows = papers, columns = fields. Would you rather have it the other way round, or switch?
  6. Q6 Exports: one column per field plus a status column each? Should quotes and page numbers go into the export too?
  7. Q7 "Correct" makes you pick the supporting quote from the text; a value with no quote can't be saved. Is that too strict for fields like "limitations"?
