Wireframe walkthrough

Three screens are drafted here before anyone builds them (plan.md rule 32). Each one is clickable: the buttons, keyboard shortcuts and approvals work, but the papers and text are invented and nothing is saved. Reload a page to start it over.

For each screen we want to hear, in your own words, three things:

  1. Where do I approve?
  2. Where do I see the source?
  3. How do I go back?

The orange Q1 pins mark the other open questions; they are listed at the bottom of each screen. Untick "Show walkthrough questions" to see the screen without them. "View as" in the dark bar switches between the owner and the supervisor, so you can see who approves what.

Suggested tasks (about 20 minutes)

Think aloud while you do them. The person taking notes should not help unless you are stuck for more than a minute; being stuck is a finding.

  1. Screening. Screen the first three records using only the keyboard. Exclude one and give a reason. Then change your mind about the last one. Find out why the AI suggested "maybe" for the telework/household record. Look at where the editorial was found. Finish the queue and the audit, resolve the conflict, and get G3 approved.
  2. Evidence table. Find the sample size of the Das & Varghese study and check it against the paper. Find the cell whose quote isn't in the paper and fix it. Get G4 approved.
  3. Claim–evidence. Find the sentence that would stop the export and fix it. Give the sentence about "who gets remote-capable jobs" its evidence. Open one piece of evidence in the evidence table, then come back to the same sentence. Approve the section.
