Privacy and your data

Manage the data held for your account. Project permissions and research decisions remain in each project workspace.

Privacy notice

This installation sends some of your project data to the outside services listed here, and only these. A service appears in the list only while it is switched on.

Data kept on this server—your account, notes, projects, files and audit log—is not listed here. You can download a copy of your own data or erase your account below.

Processors currently enabled

  • AI model providerPurpose: drafting suggestions and research answers · Host: llm.example.testData sent: selected project text needed for the requested model operation
  • OpenAlexPurpose: scholarly search · Host: api.openalex.orgData sent: search queries and record identifiers

Example data only. The live endpoint is GET /api/privacy/processors; it returns the notice and enabled processor register without credentials.

Account actions

These controls affect your account across every project. They do not approve gates or change project records.

Download my dataSave the JSON export of your personal data. This starts a download and does not delete anything.
Sign out everywhereEnd all browser sessions, including this one. You can sign in again later.
Erase my accountPermanently pseudonymise your account, remove external identities and scrub private unfiled notes. Shared-project ownership must be handed over first.
Permanent action

Erasure cannot be undone. Type your account email to confirm. The live endpoint is POST /api/auth/erase-account with {"confirm_email": "…"}.
